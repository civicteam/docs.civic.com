title: Connect Slack and other outputs

Bryn's work is invisible until it lands somewhere your team already looks.
Outputs are the destinations Plays deliver to. Slack is the fastest first
output; CRM and webhooks follow once alerts are flowing.

Outputs live under **Integrations** in the Bryn dashboard, and the
onboarding wizard walks you through the first one.

## Connect Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Click **Connect to Slack** and approve the app in your Slack
    workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a channel">
    A dedicated channel like `#intent` keeps alerts easy to follow and
    easy to mute-proof. The Bryn dashboard holds one Slack connection;
    each Play then picks its own channel in the same workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver the test message">
    Click **Deliver test message**. Bryn posts "Bryn is connected to this
    channel." and marks the connection verified when it arrives.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**You'll know it worked when** the test message is sitting in the channel
and the dashboard shows **Slack connected and verified.** If it failed,
check the app is still in your Slack workspace and click **Reverify**. The
[Slack connector page](/bryn/connectors/slack) has the full detail.

## Other destinations

- **CRM**: a Play adds the person to your CRM, matched on their email. How
  a write treats fields your team already filled in depends on the CRM, so
  check its page under [Connectors](/bryn/connectors).
- **Outgoing webhooks**: receive structured records in your own systems.
  The request format, headers and test steps are on the
  [Outgoing webhook](/bryn/connectors/outgoing-webhook) page.

## Keeping it quiet

Alerts you mute are worse than no alerts, so Bryn rate-limits itself:

- **Cooldowns** stop the same Play re-firing on the same account for a
  configurable window (a week by default).
- **Workspace caps** keep total alert volume bounded even during a traffic
  spike.

If a channel feels noisy, tighten the Play's trigger or your
[Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)](/bryn/icp-and-scoring) rather than muting
the channel.
