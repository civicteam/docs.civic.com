Zoho CRM is Zoho's customer relationship manager, where your team keeps
leads, contacts, accounts and deals. Bryn connects to your Zoho CRM org
with one sign-in and uses it three ways.

Zoho CRM works with Bryn in three modes: **Input** (new leads and contacts
arrive as signals), **Enrichment** (Bryn looks people and their accounts up
in your CRM) and **Output** (a Play adds the person to your CRM as a lead or
a contact).

## How Bryn integrates Zoho CRM

You connect Zoho CRM with OAuth: there is no API key to paste. Under
[**Integrations**](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations) in the Bryn
dashboard, choose **Zoho CRM**. Bryn sends you to Zoho to approve access,
then brings you back and checks it can reach your CRM. On that connect
form you also choose:

- **Use Zoho CRM for enrichment.** On by default. Each lookup spends API
  credits from your Zoho CRM org's daily allowance. You can change this
  later in **Configuration**, under **Enrichment**.
- **Also send me new leads and contacts.** Tick this and Bryn sets up the
  Input side for you as soon as the connection is made (see
  [Input](#input)).

Plays can add people to your Zoho CRM as soon as the connection is
verified; there is nothing else to switch on for Output.

A few things to know about the connection:

- **Sign in as an administrator if you want Input.** Bryn asks for read
  access to leads, contacts, accounts and deals, write access to leads and
  contacts, and permission to manage webhooks and workflow rules. Only a
  Zoho CRM user who can manage workflow rules can let Bryn set up the
  Input side.
- **Your data centre is detected for you.** A Zoho org lives in one data
  centre (US, EU, India, Australia, Japan, UK, Canada, Saudi Arabia, UAE
  or Singapore). Zoho tells Bryn which one when you approve access, and
  Bryn only ever sends your token to that data centre's Zoho addresses.
  Zoho's China data centre isn't supported. The Bryn dashboard shows the
  connection under your org's name, with its data centre beside it.
- **One connection at a time.** The Bryn dashboard holds one Zoho CRM
  connection at a time: once it is connected, Zoho CRM no longer appears
  in the list of connectors to add. Connecting the same org again replaces
  the stored grant and revokes the old one in Zoho, so reconnecting
  never piles up tokens against Zoho's limit of 20 per user.
- **Bryn keeps only the refresh token.** Short-lived access tokens are
  minted when needed and never stored.

### How Bryn verifies the connection

Right after you approve access, Bryn reads the connecting user's own
profile from Zoho CRM. This writes nothing. If Zoho answers, the connection
shows as verified. If not, the Bryn dashboard shows **I couldn't reach your
Zoho CRM. Try connecting it again.**

### Disconnecting

When you disconnect Zoho CRM, Bryn first removes the webhooks and workflow
rules it created for your Zoho CRM sources (while it still has access),
then revokes its token in Zoho and deletes the stored credential.

## Input

### What arrives

A Zoho CRM source in your
[Inputs](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?view=inputs) receives
every new lead and contact that has an email address:

| Zoho CRM event | Bryn signal | When |
|---|---|---|
| `lead.created` | `crm_lead_created` | A lead is created in Zoho CRM |
| `contact.created` | `crm_contact_created` | A contact is created in Zoho CRM |

Records without an email are not sent. Zoho retries a failed delivery once,
after 15 minutes, and Bryn uses the record's ID so the retry is never
counted twice.

These are recorded as CRM events, not as a visitor identifying themselves:
a new CRM record is often a rep's entry or an import.

### How records match accounts and people

- **Person**: matched by email, with first name, last name and title
  (a lead's **Designation**, a contact's **Title**).
- **Account**: a lead is placed using its **Website** and **Company**,
  falling back to the email's domain. A contact carries no company in the
  webhook, so its account comes from the email's domain.

From there the signals count towards the account's
[score](/bryn/icp-and-scoring) and can trigger [Plays](/bryn/plays). To
change which signal an event raises, see
[Signal mapping](/bryn/signal-mapping).

### Set up with your connection (quick setup)

Zoho CRM sends records to Bryn through a webhook that a workflow rule runs
when a record is created. Bryn can create these in your Zoho CRM for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Zoho CRM">
    Connect from [**Integrations**](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?connect=zoho_crm)
    and tick **Also send me new leads and contacts**, signing in as a Zoho
    CRM administrator. If Zoho CRM is already connected, go to
    [**Inputs**](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?view=inputs),
    add **Zoho CRM** and choose **Set up with Zoho CRM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what Bryn created">
    Bryn adds a Zoho CRM source to your Inputs and, in Zoho CRM, a webhook
    and a workflow rule for leads and another pair for contacts. They are
    named after the source, so you can find them in Zoho CRM under
    **Setup**, **Automation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a test lead">
    Create a lead with an email address in Zoho CRM. It shows up in Bryn
    within a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If Zoho refuses the setup, Bryn keeps the source and tells you why. The
common reasons:

- **Webhooks need Zoho CRM Professional or above**, and each edition has
  a daily webhook limit.
- **The connecting user can't manage workflow rules.** Reconnect as an
  administrator.
- **The connection predates Input.** A connection made before Bryn asked
  for webhook and workflow permissions must be reconnected first.

You can run quick setup again at any time from the source's drawer
(**Set up or replace the Zoho CRM webhooks**). Running it again replaces
the webhooks and workflow rules: Bryn creates the new ones before deleting
the old, so nothing is missed in between.

### Set it up yourself

Choose **I'd rather set it up myself** when adding Zoho CRM, or **Set it up
yourself** on the new source. The dashboard shows the steps with your
endpoint URL, credential and request bodies filled in. For each of
**Leads** and **Contacts**:

1. In Zoho CRM, go to **Setup**, **Automation**, **Actions**, **Webhooks**
   and configure a webhook: method **POST**, your endpoint URL, the module,
   and authorization type **General**.
2. Under **Headers**, add a custom parameter named `Authorization` with the
   value shown in the Bryn dashboard. Zoho also needs one module
   parameter before it saves custom headers: add `X-Zoho-Record-Id`
   holding the record's ID.
3. Under **Body**, choose **Raw**, then **JSON**, and paste the body the
   dashboard shows for that module.
4. Go to **Setup**, **Automation**, **Workflow Rules**, create a rule for
   the module that runs on **Create**, with the condition **Email is not
   empty** and your webhook as its instant action.

Then create a lead with an email address and check it arrives.

### Keeping it working

- **Keep the people your Plays add from counting twice.** A Play that adds
  someone to Zoho CRM creates a lead or contact, which then comes back in
  as a signal. Set that Play's lead source to `Bryn` (see
  [Output](#output)) and the source skips those records.
- **After you rotate the source's key**, Bryn replaces the webhooks it set
  up with ones carrying the new key. If you set them up by hand, update the
  `Authorization` header in both webhooks.
- **Deleting the source removes its webhooks and workflow rules** from
  Zoho CRM.

## Enrichment

With enrichment on, Bryn looks people up in your Zoho CRM by email: first
among contacts, then among leads. A lookup is reused for a day before Bryn
asks Zoho again.

**About the person**, when your CRM knows them:

- whether they are a **contact** or a **lead**
- their title, the Zoho CRM user who owns the record, the lead status and
  the lead source

**About the account**, when the person is a contact linked to a Zoho CRM
account:

- the account's name, website, industry, employee count, annual revenue
  and billing country
- the account owner
- the number of open deals, with up to 10 of them

A lead has no account in Zoho CRM, so a match on a lead only fills in the
person. Results appear under **Zoho CRM** on the account and the person in
Bryn, and Plays can use them. Employee count, country and website also
feed the account's main details. Zoho's search index isn't real time, so a
record created moments ago may not be found until the next lookup.

## Output

A Play can add the person it fired for to your Zoho CRM. In the Play's
Zoho CRM step, pick your connection and choose:

- **Lead or contact.** By default Bryn adds the person as a lead. Turn on
  the contact option to add them as a contact instead.
- **Lead source** (optional): the value to record, for example `Bryn`.
  It supports `{{variable}}` substitution. Leave it empty to leave the
  field unset.

What lands in Zoho CRM:

- **As a lead**: email, first and last name, company, title
  (**Designation**), website and lead source. When Bryn doesn't know the
  company's name, it uses the company's domain, because Zoho's default
  lead layout requires a company.
- **As a contact**: email, first and last name, title and lead source.
  Bryn doesn't create or link Zoho CRM accounts.

Writes are careful with the data you already have:

- **Matched on email.** If the person is already there, Bryn updates that
  record instead of creating a second one.
- **Blanks never overwrite.** Empty fields are left out of the write.
- **No duplicate leads for customers.** When adding a lead, Bryn first
  checks whether the person is already a contact, and leaves them alone if
  so.
- **No placeholders.** A person without an email or a last name is skipped
  rather than written with made-up details.
- **Your workflow rules still run** on the records Bryn creates or updates.

### How to tell it worked

The Play's test button checks that Zoho CRM is reachable and adds nothing:
it reports **Zoho CRM is reachable, nothing was added**. Each run shows its
outcome in the Play's activity: **Added the person to your CRM**,
**Updated the person in your CRM**, **Already a contact in your CRM, so I
didn't add a lead**, or **Couldn't add the person to your CRM**.
