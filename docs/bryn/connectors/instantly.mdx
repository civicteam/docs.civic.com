Instantly is a cold email platform that sends multi-step outreach campaigns
from your own mailboxes. Bryn works with Instantly in two modes on one API
key: **Output**, where a [Play](/bryn/plays) adds a person to one of your
Instantly campaigns as a lead, and **Enrichment**, where Bryn checks whether
a person is already enrolled in any of your campaigns.

Bryn uses your own Instantly workspace, so every lead Bryn adds and every
lookup it makes runs on your Instantly plan and API limits.

## How Bryn integrates Instantly

You connect Instantly with an API key under
[**Integrations**](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations) in the Bryn
dashboard. One connection serves both modes: Plays use the key to add leads
to campaigns, and the enricher uses the same key to read which campaigns a
person is in.

<Warning>
**Create the key with the `leads:all` and `campaigns:read` scopes.** Bryn
needs `leads:all` to add leads and look them up, and `campaigns:read` to list
your campaigns and check the one a Play targets. Don't use an `all:all` key:
Bryn does not need that much access.
</Warning>

When you enter the key, Bryn checks it straight away by listing your
campaigns:

- **A rejected key** shows an error beside the field and you cannot connect it.
- **A key without `campaigns:read`** shows an error asking for a key with the
  `campaigns:read` and `leads:all` scopes.
- **A workspace without an active paid plan** shows an error, since
  Instantly's API is not available on it.
- **A valid key** loads your campaigns so you can pick the connection's
  **Default campaign**. If the workspace has no campaigns yet, create one in
  Instantly first, then enter the key again.

If Instantly itself cannot be reached at that moment, the form lets you paste
the default campaign's id instead, so an Instantly outage does not block you.

After you connect, Bryn verifies the connection by reading the default
campaign from Instantly. This needs a valid key and a campaign that exists, so
a mistyped campaign id fails here rather than on the first delivery. Verifying
adds no lead and changes nothing in the campaign. Bryn keeps the key in
encrypted storage; it is never shown again, logged, or passed to Plays.

The Bryn dashboard holds one Instantly connection at a time: once it is
connected, Instantly no longer appears in the list of connectors to add. To
change the key or the default campaign, disconnect Instantly and connect
again.

## Enrichment

Bryn checks, person by person, whether someone is already a lead in one of
your Instantly campaigns. This is a read only lookup: enrichment never adds,
changes or removes anything in Instantly.

- **What Bryn looks up**: the person's own email address. A person without
  an email is skipped, and checked as soon as an email arrives. Bryn never
  uses a colleague's address from the same account.
- **What Bryn reads**: every campaign the email is enrolled in, across all
  your campaigns, not only the connection's default. For each campaign Bryn
  reads its name, the lead's status in it (Active, Paused, Completed,
  Bounced, Unsubscribed or Skipped), open and reply counts, when the lead was
  added and when it was last contacted. A lead that sits in an Instantly
  list but not in a campaign does not count.
- **How it is used**: the result sets the person's **In an outreach
  campaign** state, which a Play trigger can test under **Person**. It stays
  true between checks and clears when the person leaves every campaign. Use it
  to act on a signup from someone your outbound team is already emailing, or
  to keep a Play away from people who are already in a campaign.

**When it runs.** Bryn checks a person when an event about them arrives, at
most once every five minutes per person, so a membership change in Instantly
shows up in Bryn within a few minutes.

**Turning it on and off.** The connect form asks whether to **Use Instantly
for enrichment**. The switch is on by default. You can change it later in
**Configuration**, under **Enrichment**, without disconnecting Instantly, so
Plays keep delivering either way.

**When Instantly refuses.** If Instantly rejects the key during a lookup,
Bryn marks the connection as failed so you can fix it under **Integrations**.
If Instantly is busy or unreachable, Bryn retries the lookup.

## Output

A Play can add the person it acts on to an Instantly campaign as a lead.
Instantly then emails them on the campaign's own schedule and steps, from the
mailboxes the campaign uses.

Bryn sends the person's email address (required, since Instantly leads are
keyed by email), and where known their first name, last name and company
name. A person with no email address is recorded as a skipped delivery.

Bryn asks Instantly to skip anyone who is already in that campaign, so a
repeat delivery never adds the person twice. It still counts as delivered,
because the person is in the campaign either way.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API key in Instantly">
    In your Instantly workspace settings, create an API key with the
    `leads:all` and `campaigns:read` scopes. Make sure the workspace is on a
    paid plan and has at least one campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Instantly in the Bryn dashboard">
    [Open the Instantly connect form](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?connect=instantly)
    (or go to **Integrations** and choose **Instantly**) and paste the key.
    Pick the **Default campaign** new leads go to unless a Play names
    another one. Leave **Use Instantly for enrichment** on if you want the
    campaign check described above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Instantly to a Play">
    In the Play's outputs, add Instantly and pick the connection. Leave the
    campaign empty to use the connection's default, or pick another of your
    campaigns for this Play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the entry">
    Click **Test** on the Instantly entry. Bryn checks that the key works
    and the campaign is reachable, without adding anyone to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A Play only delivers to Instantly once the connection is verified. If it is
not, or no campaign is set, the delivery is recorded as skipped rather than
failed.

**You'll know it worked when** the person appears as a lead in the Instantly
campaign, with their name and company filled in where Bryn knew them.
