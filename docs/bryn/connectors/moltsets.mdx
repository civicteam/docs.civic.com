MoltSets is a contact and company data platform with an API built for
agents. Bryn uses MoltSets in one mode, **Enrichment**: with your own
MoltSets API key, Bryn looks up the business profile behind a person's
email address and the company behind an account's domain, and can
discover key people at an account. [Scoring](/bryn/icp-and-scoring) and
your [Plays](/bryn/plays) then use what it finds.

MoltSets is never a delivery destination. It does not appear in any
Play's outputs, and Bryn writes nothing to your MoltSets account.

## How Bryn integrates MoltSets

You connect MoltSets with a MoltSets API key (it starts with `ms_`) under
[**Integrations**](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations) in the Bryn
dashboard. The key is the only thing Bryn needs. Bryn has no MoltSets key
of its own, so connecting your account is the only way to enrich with
MoltSets. You can have one MoltSets connection at a time.

<Note>
**Lookups count toward your MoltSets plan.** Every lookup Bryn makes uses
your key and draws on your plan's fair use limits. Bryn looks each person
and each account up at most once every 30 days, and pauses when your
allowance for the current window runs out, so it does not keep spending
requests MoltSets will refuse.
</Note>

When you connect, Bryn checks the key by reading your MoltSets account
details. That check is free and uses none of your allowance. The
connection only counts as verified when MoltSets accepts the key **and**
reports the account as active. If the key is valid but the account is not
active, Bryn asks you to check your plan in MoltSets and verify again.
Bryn keeps the key in encrypted storage; it is never shown again, logged,
or passed to Plays.

The connect form asks two questions, both on by default:

- **Use MoltSets for enrichment.** Bryn starts looking people and
  accounts up once the key is verified. If you turn it off, the
  connection is saved but no lookups run until you turn it on.
- **Discover people.** When Bryn enriches an account, it also looks up
  key people who work there (see [Key contacts](#key-contacts) below).
  This switch is only offered while enrichment is on.

You can change both later on the
[MoltSets card](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?connector=provider%3Amoltsets)
in **Integrations**, or under **Configuration**, in **Enrichment**.
Disconnect MoltSets to remove the key altogether.

## Enrichment

MoltSets enrichment works at two levels, and Bryn looks each one up on
its own.

**People.** When Bryn knows a person's own email address, it asks
MoltSets for the business profile behind it. Bryn keeps the person's
first and last name, job title, seniority, functional area, LinkedIn URL,
country, company name and domain, and their confirmed business email with
its risk score and validation date. The job title becomes the person's
title in Bryn. Personal email addresses are never kept.

The risk score is a send safety grade from MoltSets, not a check that the
address belongs to the person: A means validated deliverable, B means
known engagement, C means a catch-all domain, D means negative evidence
(such as a spam trap or complaint), and F means no signal.

**Accounts.** When Bryn knows an account's domain, it asks MoltSets for
the company with that domain. Bryn keeps the company name, domain, size
band, employee count, country, city and state, LinkedIn URL, revenue
range, NAICS codes, and logo. The industry comes from MoltSets as a
LinkedIn industry name, which Bryn maps to its standard industry list, so
it feeds the industry part of your Fit score. Bryn only keeps a company
whose domain matches the one it asked about.

When a lookup completes and MoltSets has no match, Bryn removes any
earlier MoltSets answer for that person or account, so a stale answer
stops counting.

**When it runs.** MoltSets runs as part of Bryn's normal enrichment when
an account or person produces a new [signal](/bryn/signal-mapping). A
person without an email address, or an account without a domain, is
skipped for that half of the lookup. Each person and each account is
looked up at most once per 30 days, including lookups that found nothing.

**Where results appear.** Open an account in the Bryn dashboard: MoltSets
is listed under **Enrichment sources** with what it filled and when, and
the fields it filled show on the account and its people.

### Key contacts

With **Discover people** on, Bryn also asks MoltSets for key people at
the account's domain when it enriches the account. By default Bryn adds
up to 10 people. It skips anyone without a business email, and the
person the signal was already about.

Each key contact is added under the account as a discovered person:
someone who has not interacted with you yet. Discovered people are not
treated as contactable by default, and Bryn skips discovery for accounts
and people in the EU. A discovered person who never interacts with you is
removed 90 days after Bryn last found them; one who later identifies
themselves (for example by filling in a form) becomes an ordinary person
in Bryn. Deleting or suppressing a person in Bryn covers discovered
people too. Bryn asks for an account's key contacts at most once per 30
days.

Key contact searches draw on your MoltSets search allowance, which is
usually the smallest part of a plan. Turn **Discover people** off if you
want to keep that allowance for your own use.

### Plans, limits and errors

MoltSets meters plans in records and requests over rolling 5 hour and
weekly windows, with separate allowances for email lookups and for
searches. Bryn reads the remaining allowance from every answer:

- **Allowance used up.** When a window runs out, Bryn pauses that kind of
  lookup until the window resets. People and accounts that were due are
  looked up on their next pass after the reset; nothing is marked as done
  in the meantime.
- **Not on your plan.** If MoltSets says your plan does not cover a
  lookup (or your free search trial is used up), Bryn stops asking for
  that kind of lookup for 6 hours. The connection stays verified, because
  the key itself is fine.
- **Key rejected or account inactive.** Bryn marks the connection as
  needing attention on the
  [MoltSets card](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?connector=provider%3Amoltsets).
  Reconnect with a working key, or reactivate your MoltSets account and
  verify again, to resume.
- **Rate limits and brief outages** are retried automatically.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a MoltSets API key">
    In the [MoltSets app](https://app.moltsets.com), create an API key. It
    starts with `ms_`. Make sure your MoltSets account is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect MoltSets in the Bryn dashboard">
    [Open the MoltSets connect form](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?connect=moltsets)
    (or go to **Integrations** and choose **MoltSets**), paste the key,
    leave **Use MoltSets for enrichment** on, choose whether to
    **Discover people**, and connect. Bryn checks the key with MoltSets
    before saving the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that it worked">
    Wait for a new signal on an account that has a domain, then open the
    account. MoltSets should be listed under **Enrichment sources**, with
    the company's size, industry and country filled in.
  </Step>
</Steps>
