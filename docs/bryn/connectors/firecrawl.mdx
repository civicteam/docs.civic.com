Firecrawl is a web scraping API that turns websites into clean, structured
data. Bryn uses your Firecrawl account to read the companies in your
pipeline from their own websites.

Firecrawl works in two modes in Bryn:

- **Enrichment**: Bryn reads each account's homepage and site map, and
  keeps a short description, the page title, the favicon, and the
  addresses of its pricing, careers, changelog and news pages.
- **Input**: if you switch it on, Bryn creates a Firecrawl page monitor
  for each of your best-fit accounts. When a watched page changes in a way
  that matters to a salesperson, Firecrawl tells Bryn, and Bryn raises a
  `website_change` [signal](/bryn/signal-mapping) on the account.

Firecrawl is never a delivery destination: it does not appear in any
Play's outputs.

## How Bryn integrates Firecrawl

You connect Firecrawl with a Firecrawl API key under
[**Integrations**](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations) in the Bryn
dashboard. One connection covers both modes: the enricher and the page
monitors use the same key. The Bryn dashboard holds one Firecrawl
connection at a time: once it is connected, Firecrawl no longer appears in
the list of connectors to add.

<Note>
**Everything runs on your Firecrawl credits.** Bryn has no shared
Firecrawl key, so every scrape, map and monitor check is billed to the
Firecrawl team that owns the key you connect. Firecrawl keys belong to a
team, so create the key in the team whose credits you want Bryn to spend.
</Note>

When you connect, Bryn checks the key by reading your team's credit usage
from Firecrawl. That check scrapes nothing, so it costs no credits. The
connection only counts as verified once Firecrawl accepts the key, and you
can check it again at any time with **Verify** in the Firecrawl drawer.
Bryn keeps the key in encrypted storage; it is never shown again, logged,
or passed to Plays.

The connect form has one more switch, **Watch your best-fit accounts'
websites**, which turns on the Input mode. It is off by default, because
every watched account spends credits every day. You can change it later in
the **Settings** section of the Firecrawl drawer, without reconnecting.

**Disconnecting** deletes every page monitor Bryn created in your
Firecrawl account, switches off the Firecrawl input source, and removes
the key. The same clean-up runs when you switch monitoring off, or when
you reconnect to change keys. If Firecrawl can't be reached for some of
those deletes, Bryn refuses the change and keeps the key, because only
that key can remove the remaining monitors. Try again a few minutes later.

## Enrichment

Firecrawl enrichment is **account level only**. It reads a company's own
website, so it learns nothing about people.

**What Bryn reads.** For an account with a domain, Bryn makes two calls:

- A **scrape** of the homepage, asking Firecrawl for a summary of the
  page. Bryn keeps the summary as the account's description (up to 300
  characters), the page title, and the site's favicon. If the site
  answered with an error page or a bot challenge, Bryn keeps nothing from
  the scrape, so that page never becomes the company's description.
- A **map** of the site, which lists its links. From those, Bryn picks the
  account's **pricing**, **careers**, **changelog** and **news** pages,
  where the site has them. Only links on the company's own site count (an
  off-site job board is ignored), and the shortest match wins, so
  `/pricing` beats `/pricing/enterprise`.

Either call alone still counts: a site whose scrape failed but whose map
worked keeps its pages. Each call costs one credit, so an account costs
about 2 credits per lookup.

**Where results appear.** Open an account in the Bryn dashboard: Firecrawl
is listed under **Enrichment sources** with what it found and when. The
description and favicon also compete for the account's main description
and logo. Firecrawl only fills a description that no other source has
set, so it never overwrites one. Firecrawl does not set the company name,
industry, size, location or revenue; [Clay](/bryn/connectors/clay) and
[Tavily](/bryn/connectors/tavily) cover those.

**When it runs.** Bryn looks an account up when it produces a new signal,
or when a mapped source first creates it, and then at most once every 30
days. An account without a domain is skipped until a domain arrives.

**When Firecrawl refuses.** If Firecrawl rejects the key, Bryn marks the
connection as failed; reconnect with a working key to resume. If your
Firecrawl team runs out of credits, lookups fail and are retried within
the hour, so accounts fill in once you top up. Rate limits, timeouts and
brief outages are retried automatically.

The pages the enricher finds are also what the page monitors watch.
Without Firecrawl enrichment switched on, a monitor watches the homepage
only.

## Input

With **Watch your best-fit accounts' websites** on, Bryn uses Firecrawl's
page monitoring to watch the websites of the accounts that matter most
to you, and turns meaningful changes into signals.

**Which accounts are watched.** Bryn creates a monitor the next time it
scores an account that:

- has a domain;
- has a Fit score at or above **Lowest Fit score to watch** (75 by
  default; you can set 50 to 100);
- hasn't converted;
- fits under **Most accounts to watch at once** (25 by default, up to
  200).

Nothing is created at connect time. Bryn pauses an account's monitor when
its Fit score drops below your bar or it converts, and resumes it if the
Fit score reaches the bar again. A new bar or ceiling applies as accounts
are next scored.

**What a monitor watches.** Each monitor covers the account's homepage
plus the pricing, careers, changelog and news pages the enricher found. It
checks daily at 06:00 UTC. Firecrawl judges each change against a goal
Bryn sets: pricing, plans or packaging; hiring and open roles; product
launches and releases; leadership; funding, acquisitions and other company
news. Cosmetic, navigation, layout, cookie banner and timestamp changes
are ignored. You'll see the monitors in your Firecrawl dashboard, each
named `Bryn:` followed by the account's domain.

**How events reach Bryn.** When monitoring is on, Bryn sets up a
**Firecrawl** source in the Inputs lane of Integrations for you. It reads
"Set up by your Firecrawl connection" and has no switch of its own: the
Firecrawl connection turns it on and off. Every monitor posts a
`monitor.page` webhook to that source for each page it checks, carrying
the account's domain, so the event always lands on the account the
monitor was created for, whichever page changed. A webhook for a domain
Bryn doesn't already have never creates an account.

**What becomes a signal.** Bryn raises `website_change` on the account
only when Firecrawl's judge calls the change meaningful. The first check
records each page as it is, so nothing is raised until a later check finds
a change. Trivial or unchanged pages arrive as events with no signal. The
event behind each `website_change` carries Firecrawl's reasoning, each
meaningful change with its before and after, and the diff. A
[Play](/bryn/plays) that triggers on `website_change` acts on it.

**Cost.** Each page check costs about one credit, plus one when a changed
page is judged: roughly 150 to 200 credits per watched account per month.
With the default ceiling of 25 accounts, that is up to about 5,000
credits a month.

<Tip>
You don't need to set anything up in Firecrawl for the Input mode. Bryn
creates the monitors with your key, points them at your Firecrawl source,
and keeps their webhook secret up to date when you rotate it.
</Tip>

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Firecrawl API key">
    In the Firecrawl dashboard, pick the team whose credits Bryn should
    spend in the team switcher, open **API Keys**, and create a key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Firecrawl in the Bryn dashboard">
    [Open the Firecrawl connect form](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?connect=firecrawl)
    (or go to **Integrations** and choose **Firecrawl**) and paste the
    key. To use the Input mode, switch on **Watch your best-fit accounts'
    websites** and set the lowest Fit score and the most accounts to
    watch. Bryn checks the key with Firecrawl before saving the
    connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on Firecrawl enrichment">
    On the [Firecrawl card](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?connector=provider%3Afirecrawl)
    in the Enrichment lane, turn on **Firecrawl enrichment**. A connection
    on its own enriches nothing, and without enrichment the monitors
    watch homepages only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that it worked">
    Wait for a new signal on an account that has a domain, then open the
    account: Firecrawl should be listed under **Enrichment sources**. If
    monitoring is on, a qualifying account's monitor appears in your
    Firecrawl dashboard the next time the account is scored, and
    `website_change` signals follow once a later check finds a meaningful
    change.
  </Step>
</Steps>
