title: Connectors
description: 'The third-party tools Bryn reads from, learns from, and delivers to'
---

A connector links Bryn to a tool your team already uses. Every connector
works in one or more of three modes, in the order data moves through Bryn:

- **Input**: the tool sends events into Bryn. Bryn turns them into
  [signals](/bryn/signal-mapping) and stitches them to the same accounts
  and people the [pixel](/bryn/pixel) sees.
- **Enrichment**: Bryn looks accounts and people up in the tool to fill in
  what it knows about them. Enrichment feeds [scoring](/bryn/icp-and-scoring)
  and gives Plays more to work with. It never delivers anything.
- **Output**: a [Play](/bryn/plays) delivers to the tool: an alert in a
  chat channel, a contact in a CRM, a person enrolled in a sequence, a
  ticket, an email, or a webhook call.

You connect each one under **Integrations** in the Bryn dashboard. Each
connector's page covers what Bryn does with it in every mode it supports,
how to connect it, and where to find the vendor's own documentation.
