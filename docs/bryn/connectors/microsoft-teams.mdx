Microsoft Teams is Microsoft's chat and meetings app for work. Bryn connects
to Teams as an **Output** connector: you give Bryn the URL of a Teams
Workflows webhook, and Bryn posts its alerts into the channel that workflow
delivers to, as a simple card.

Teams supports one mode in Bryn: Output, for chat alerts.

## How Bryn integrates Microsoft Teams

There is no OAuth app and no API key. In Teams you create a workflow that
posts to a channel whenever it receives a webhook request, and Teams gives
you a URL for it. That URL is the whole connection: whoever holds it can post
to the channel, so Bryn treats it as a secret.

You set it up under [**Integrations**](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations)
in the Bryn dashboard, in a form with one field, **Workflow webhook URL**.
Bryn stores the URL as a secret and keeps no other settings for the
connection. The dashboard never shows the URL again, so the connection is
listed as Microsoft Teams with its status, without a channel name.

Bryn only accepts a URL that:

- uses `https`, and
- lives on a Microsoft webhook host: `*.logic.azure.com` (a Teams Workflows
  or Power Automate webhook) or `*.webhook.office.com` (a classic Teams
  incoming webhook).

Any other address is rejected with **The Teams webhook URL is not valid.**
before anything is saved.

The Bryn dashboard holds one Microsoft Teams connection at a time: once it is
connected, Microsoft Teams no longer appears in the list of connectors to add.
To post to a different channel, disconnect it and connect again with the new
workflow's URL. Saving the same URL again updates the existing connection
instead of creating a duplicate, and sets it back to unverified until it is
checked again. Disconnecting removes the stored URL.

### Workflows and classic incoming webhooks

Microsoft is retiring the classic Office 365 connectors in Teams, including
the original **Incoming Webhook** connector, in favour of webhooks created
with the Workflows app. Use a Workflows webhook for new connections. Bryn
still accepts a classic incoming webhook URL if you already have one, but
plan to replace it before Microsoft switches it off.

Bryn sends the same card to both kinds of URL, so nothing else changes when
you switch.

### How Bryn verifies the connection

Teams webhooks have no read-only check, so Bryn verifies the connection by
posting a real test card to your channel right after you connect. The card
reads "Bryn is connected to Microsoft Teams."

If Teams accepts it, the Bryn dashboard shows **Test message delivered.** If
not, it shows **The Teams webhook could not be verified.** and you can fix the
workflow and test again. Bryn waits up to five seconds for Teams to answer,
so a workflow that is turned off, deleted or very slow fails the check.

Only a verified connection receives alerts.

## Output

Teams is a chat destination. When a Play's alert step fires, for example
when a website visitor matches your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), Bryn posts
one message about it to your Teams channel through the workflow.

What lands in Teams:

- **One card per alert**, in the channel your workflow posts to. The card is
  an [Adaptive Card](https://adaptivecards.io/) with a single block of
  wrapped text.
- **Plain text only.** The Slack alert layout, with its header, score fields
  and buttons back to the Bryn dashboard, is not sent to Teams. You get the
  same alert sentence without the extras.
- **Posted by the workflow**, so Teams shows it under the identity your
  workflow posts as, not as a Bryn user.

Bryn sends each alert to one chat destination only. If you have more than one
chat connector connected and verified, Bryn picks them in this order: Slack,
then Microsoft Teams, then Discord. So alerts reach Teams when it is your
only verified chat connection, or when you have no verified Slack
connection.

<Note>
  Teams receives alerts from the Play alert step. The newer Play steps that
  let you pick a specific connection per Play (for example a Slack channel
  per Play) don't list Microsoft Teams yet.
</Note>

Cooldowns and workspace caps apply to Teams alerts as they do to Slack; see
[Keeping it quiet](/bryn/outputs#keeping-it-quiet).

### Set up the connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Workflows webhook in Teams">
    In Teams, open **Workflows** and pick the template that posts to a
    channel when a webhook request is received. Choose the team and
    channel, finish the workflow, and copy the URL Teams shows you. A
    dedicated channel keeps alerts easy to follow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect in Bryn">
    [Open the Microsoft Teams connect form](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?connect=teams)
    in the Bryn dashboard and paste the URL into **Workflow webhook URL**.
    If you already have a Teams connection, the link opens
    **Integrations** without the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the test card arrive">
    Bryn posts the test card as soon as you connect. No extra step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**You'll know it worked when** the dashboard shows **Test message
delivered.** and the "Bryn is connected to Microsoft Teams." card is in your
channel. If it failed, check that you copied the whole URL and that the
workflow is turned on, then test again.
