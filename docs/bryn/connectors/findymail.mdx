Findymail finds and verifies B2B email addresses and phone numbers, and
watches LinkedIn for buying signals. Bryn uses Findymail in two modes:

- **Enrichment**: when Bryn knows a person but not their email address, it
  asks Findymail for their verified work email, from their LinkedIn profile
  or their name at the company's domain. You can also have Bryn look up
  phone numbers.
- **Input**: your Findymail Signals monitors (post engagement, keyword
  mentions, new hires, job changes, company hiring) send each signal they
  find to Bryn, where it becomes a [signal](/bryn/signal-mapping) on the
  matching account and person.

Findymail is never a delivery destination. It does not appear in any Play's
outputs, and Bryn writes nothing to your Findymail account.

## How Bryn integrates Findymail

The two modes are set up separately, under
[**Integrations**](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations) in the Bryn
dashboard:

- **Enrichment** uses your Findymail API key. You connect it once; Bryn
  holds one Findymail connection per workspace, and connecting again
  replaces the key.
- **Input** uses a webhook URL that Bryn gives you, which you paste into
  your Findymail monitors. It needs no API key, so you can use Signals
  without turning on enrichment, and the other way round.

<Note>
**Lookups are billed to your Findymail account.** Bryn uses the API key you
connect, and only that key, so every lookup uses your Findymail credits.
Findymail charges only when it finds something, and Bryn looks each person
up at most once every 30 days.
</Note>

When you connect the key, Bryn checks it by reading your Findymail credit
balance. That check spends nothing, and the connection only counts as
verified once Findymail accepts the key. Bryn keeps the key in encrypted
storage; it is never shown again, logged, or passed to Plays.

## Enrichment

Findymail enrichment is **person level**. Bryn asks Findymail about
individual people at your accounts, never about the company itself, so it
fills nothing on the account record.

Bryn offers two Findymail lookups. Each has its own card in the Enrichment
section of **Integrations**, with its own switch, and both are off until you
turn them on.

| Lookup | Who it runs for | What Bryn asks | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Findymail** (email) | A person with no email address yet, whose LinkedIn profile, or first and last name, Bryn knows. Looking up by name also needs the account's domain. | The LinkedIn profile first. If that finds nothing, the person's full name at the account's domain. | One finder credit per email found |
| **Findymail phone** | A person whose LinkedIn profile or email address Bryn knows | The person's LinkedIn profile. If Bryn only has their email, it first asks Findymail for the matching profile. | Ten finder credits per number found, plus one for a profile found from an email |

**What Bryn keeps.** The email lookup keeps the verified work email, and
whether it was found from the LinkedIn profile or from the name. The phone
lookup keeps the number, its line type when Findymail reports one, and the
LinkedIn profile if Bryn had to look it up.

**Filling the person's email.** When Findymail finds an address, Bryn
uses it as the person's email, but only if the person has none. Bryn never
overwrites an address the person gave you or that another source supplied.
Once a person has an email, the lookups that work from an email address
(such as other enrichment connectors) can run for them on their next pass.

**When it runs.** Bryn enriches a person when their account produces a new
[signal](/bryn/signal-mapping). People Bryn already knew before you turned
Findymail on are looked up on their next signal, not all at once. A person
who does not yet have what a lookup needs (a LinkedIn profile, a full name
and domain, or an email) is skipped until it arrives, and then the lookup
runs.

Each person is looked up once per 30 days. A lookup that found nothing also
waits 30 days before Bryn asks again; it cost you nothing. A lookup that
failed waits an hour before Bryn retries.

**When Findymail refuses.** If Findymail rejects the key (for example
because it was deleted), Bryn marks the connection as needing attention;
reconnect with a working key to resume. If your account runs out of credits
or your subscription is paused, lookups fail and are retried within the
hour, so people fill in once you top up. Findymail rate limits and brief
outages are retried automatically.

### Set up enrichment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your Findymail API key">
    In the Findymail app, open the **API** page and copy your API key. Make
    sure the account has finder credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Findymail in the Bryn dashboard">
    [Open the Findymail connect form](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?connect=findymail)
    (or go to **Integrations** and choose **Findymail**), paste the key and
    connect. Bryn checks the key with Findymail and shows whether it was
    verified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the lookups you want">
    In the Enrichment section of **Integrations**, turn on the
    **Findymail** card for work emails. Turn on **Findymail phone** too if
    you want phone numbers, keeping in mind each number costs ten credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that it worked">
    Wait for a new signal on an account with a person who has a LinkedIn
    profile but no email. Open the person: their email should be filled
    in, with Findymail listed as an enrichment source.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Input

Findymail Signals monitors watch LinkedIn for people and companies you care
about. Each monitor can send what it finds to a webhook URL, and Bryn gives
you that URL.

### What arrives

Every monitor posts one signal at a time. Bryn reads the signal type from
each delivery, so one Findymail source in Bryn covers all your monitors:

| Findymail signal type | Bryn signal | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| `post_engagement` | `content_engagement` | Someone liked or commented on a post the monitor watches |
| `keyword_mention` | `topic_mention` | Someone posted publicly about a topic you track |
| `new_hire` | `key_hire` | A tracked company hired into a relevant role |
| `job_change` | `job_change` | A tracked person moved company |
| `company_hiring` | `company_hiring` | A company is recruiting in an area you serve |

`content_engagement` means engagement with your own content only when the
monitor watches your own posts or company page, so point post engagement
monitors there.

These are trigger signals: reasons to reach out that a
[Play](/bryn/plays) can act on. They count towards an account's Timing like
any active signal, but add nothing to Intent until you give them a weight
in [scoring](/bryn/icp-and-scoring). Each signal is recorded once, so a
signal Findymail delivers twice raises nothing twice. To change which
signal a type raises, see [signal mapping](/bryn/signal-mapping).

### How signals match accounts and people

- **With a contact email**: the person is matched or created by their
  email, and the account by the email's domain. Their first and last name,
  job title and LinkedIn profile come with them, and so do the company's
  name, industry and size band.
- **Without an email**: Bryn matches the company's domain to an account you
  already have, and never creates one. There is no person. A signal about a
  company Bryn does not know is kept on the source as unmatched.

That is why the setup asks you to choose **email** as the monitor's
enrichment level: without it, Findymail sends the company but not the
person's address.

### Set up a Findymail source

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the source in Bryn">
    Go to [**Integrations**](https://bryn.civic.com/web/integrations?view=inputs)
    and add a **Findymail** input. Bryn shows you a webhook URL with the
    credentials already in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Signals in Findymail">
    Create a monitor, or edit an existing one. Any signal type works. Pick
    **email** as the enrichment level so Bryn can find the person, not just
    the company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL with credentials">
    Paste the URL Bryn showed you into the monitor's webhook URL. Use the
    URL with credentials, not the plain endpoint: Findymail cannot send a
    header, so the credentials travel in the URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the monitor">
    Use the same URL for every other monitor you want in Bryn.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**You'll know it worked when** the monitor's next signal appears as an
event on the source, attached to the matching account. There is no test
button, and monitors run on Findymail's own schedule, so the first signal
may take a few hours.

<Warning>
**The URL is the credential.** If you regenerate the source's secret in
Bryn, the URL changes. Paste the new URL into every monitor that uses it,
or their signals are refused.
</Warning>
